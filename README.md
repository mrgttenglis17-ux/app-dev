# Spirited Away - A Masterpiece by Hayao Miyazaki

Spirited Away is a **highly acclaimed** animated movie directed by **Hayao Miyazaki**. It was released in 2001 and won numerous awards, including the **Academy Award for Best Animated Feature**.

## Plot Overview
Chihiro, a young girl, finds herself trapped in a mysterious world full of spirits and creatures. As she works at a bathhouse owned by the witch Yubaba, she learns valuable lessons about courage, self-reliance, and growing up.

### Main Characters
- **Chihiro**: The main protagonist, a young girl who must navigate the spirit world.
- **Haku**: A mysterious boy who helps Chihiro.
- **Yubaba**: The witch who runs the bathhouse.

---

### Notable Themes:
- **Coming of Age**: The story focuses on Chihiro's transformation from a scared child to a courageous young adult.
- **Environmentalism**: Many themes in the movie reflect the importance of respecting nature.

---

### Memorable Quotes
> *"Once you do something, you never forget. Even if you can't remember."* - **Chihiro**

---

### Fun Facts
- **Studio Ghibli's** *Spirited Away* has grossed over $380 million worldwide.
- The film was one of the highest-grossing Japanese films ever.

I absolutely love this movie! The animation, the music, and the deep themes really make it stand out. It’s definitely one of my favorite movies of all time. :heart:
